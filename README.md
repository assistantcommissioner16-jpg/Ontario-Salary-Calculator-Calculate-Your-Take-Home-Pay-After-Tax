Ontario Salary Calculator: Calculate Your Take-Home Pay After Tax
Knowing how much of your salary you actually take home is essential for managing your finances. Your gross salary is not the same as the amount deposited into your bank account. Federal income tax, Ontario provincial income tax, Canada Pension Plan (CPP) contributions, CPP2 and Employment Insurance (EI) premiums all affect your final pay.

Our Ontario Salary Calculator 2026 helps you estimate your take-home pay after these common deductions. Enter your gross salary, choose your pay frequency and review an estimated breakdown of your income.

Whether you live in Toronto, Ottawa, Mississauga, Brampton, Hamilton, London, Windsor or another Ontario community, this calculator can help you understand how much of your employment income you may keep after taxes and payroll deductions.
